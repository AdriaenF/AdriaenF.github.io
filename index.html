About Adrian

Adriean Ferranti is a Brooklyn-based artist whose practice spans drawing, mixed media, and digital illustration. With roots in classical draftsmanship, Adriean experiments with layered narratives that uncover the tensions between human form and imagined machinery.

Recent exhibitions include independent shows in New York and collaborative pop-ups focusing on experimental sketch processes. Adriean also leads workshops on drawing for movement and creative ideation.
